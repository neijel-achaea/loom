Source: Mhaldor CLHELP (in-game city help), pasted by Ricardo 2026-10-09.
Reference for #30 (hunting attacks) and defcon tables (defensive abilities). Covers Mhaldor's classes only; ends mid-Shaman.

-----Alchemist-----
Hunting Ability: Iron, Wrack*
Defensive Ability: Robes**, Tin (damage reflection), Salt (afflictions)
and Sulphur (increase efficiency of health vials)
Weapon: None
Armor: Ringmail
Shield: Cavalry
*Wrack has potential for more damage per second, especially with the
Nimble trait, but Iron scales damage with Alchemy, which is the first
area of focus. Wrack can have its damage TYPE changed to 4 different
types if you use -- WRACK <target> <type> -- using either Choleric
(poison), Melancholic (cold), Phlegmatic (asphyxiation), or Sanguine
(magic). By default it does choleric.
**Robes can be imbued with four elements for additional effects.
Generally start with Lead, Iron, Salt, Tin.
Notes:
--> Kill a rat and use EXTISPICY to increase chances of critical
strikes.
--> Should you need to bond with the anchor, it can be found at
'Crossing a peak in the mountains' in the Northern Vashnar Mountains.
Once you reach it, TOUCH ANCHOR to attune yourself. Seek out a superior
if you need assistance getting there.
-----Apostate-----
Hunting Ability: Decay (Necromancy), then Deadeyes Bleed Bleed
Defensive Ability: Armour (Apostasy)
Weapon: Daegger (Apostasy)
Armor: Ringmail
Shield: Cavalry
-----Bard-----
Hunting Ability: Jab or Highsun
Defensive Ability: Tune, Prelude (magic resist)
Weapon: Rapier (Songblessed)*
Armor: Leather
Shield: Buckler
*You begin with a Songblessed Rapier. Any new rapier will need to be
taken to the grotto to be Songblessed once more.
Notes:
--> Compositions have various effects. Most important to hunting are
movements: Maqam for increased critical hit chance, Aria for endurance
and Willpower regeneration (useful on extended hunting trips), Scherzo
for health regeneration, and Prelude for increased magic resistance.
-----Blademaster-----
Hunting Ability: Drawslash <Target> Sternum while in a Stance*; Infuse
Fire**; Multislash much later***
Defensive Ability: Constitution, Alleviate (remove afflictions),
eventually Toughness and Weathering (in Shindo)
Weapon: TwoArts Blade****
Armor: Scalemail
Shield: None
*Blademasters fight in stances. Use Doya (Damage increase) until you
unlock Thyr (Speed). Thyr ends up more damage per second. Arash can be
useful, but while it increases damage dealt, it increases damage
received by 20%. Often the tradeoff means fleeing more often to heal,
and dying more often while hunting.
**At level 30, if you are within a house, you can EMBRACE CLASS and gain
access to Shindo. You must learn up to Shin Trance to gain shin energy,
allowing you to infuse. Uses willpower.
***Can be infused, but does not gain Shin energy. Does more damage per
second, but takes longer to recover and also uses much more endurance.
****Unlike other warriors who can buy weapons in shops, Blademasters
must obtain a named blade from Kavaya and Murad (see AB BLADENAME) a"see
Notes below.
Notes:
--> Getting your TwoArts Blade should be your first priority overall,
ahead of the skill priorities above. Seek out Kavaya and Murad, bow to
Kavaya respectfully, indicate your interest, and then bring Murad the
materials he requests. Guidance on where these can be found is in CLHELP
BLADEMASTERQUEST.
-----Depthswalker-----

Defensive Ability: Images, Shadow Cloak, Rewind (healing) and Accelerate
(afflictions)
Weapon: Scythe (Manifested - AB MANIFEST)
Armor: Scalemail
Shield: Cavalry
-----Jester-----
Hunting Ability: Bop, Gallowshumour (below 50% health)
Defensive Ability: Fool (afflictions), Priestess (healing), Magician
(mana), Sun (passive healing, short-lived)
Weapon: Blackjack*
Armor: Scalemail
Shield: Banded
*Wished for with 500g using the Props ability in Pranks.
-----Warrior (Infernal or Runewarden)-----
Hunting Ability: Varies by specialisation (see Notes)
Defensive Ability: Varies by specialisation (see Notes)
Weapon: Varies by specialisation (see Notes)
Armor: Field Plate or Full Plate*
Shield: Varies by specialisation (see Notes)
*Full Plate is the better but much more expensive option. Infernals
should hellforge their gear to strengthen it.
Notes:
--> Infernals and Runewardens operate differently from other classes
because your specialisation determines the skills and weapons you use.
--> At 23 Lessons into Weaponmastery you will learn Specialise, which
lets you choose a weapon style. Choose carefully a"changing your
specialisation afterward costs 100 lessons.
----> Two-Handed: Weapon - Warhammer or Bastard Sword; Shield - None;
Hunting Abilities - Slaughter (Warhammer) and Carve (Bastard Sword).
----> Dual Cutting: Weapon - Two Scimitars or two axes; Shield - None;
Hunting Abilities - Doubleslash and Razeslash.
----> Dual Blunt: Weapon - Two Morning Stars; Shield - None; Hunting
Abilities - Doublewhirl and Fracture.
----> Sword and Shield: Weapon - Longsword or Broadsword; Shield - Tower
Shield; Hunting Abilities - Combination (Slice and Smash).
----> Warning: Do not summon a hyena (Infernals) or falcon (Runewardens)
until you are sure you can do so. This will generally not be for some
time, until you have joined a Mhaldorian House and advanced sufficiently
through its ranks.
-----Magi-----
Hunting Ability: Firelash, Staffcast** with rapidity
Defensive Ability: Stoneskin, then Diamondskin; Reflect (absorbs one
attack)
Weapon: None***
Armor: Cloth
Shield: None
*At level 30, if you are in a house, you can EMBRACE CLASS to unlock
Artificing. At that point, save at least 23 lessons to craft your
elemental staff; beyond that, both Elementalism and Artificing are valid
next investments.
**Later on, with Artificing.
***Eventually, you will be able to create your own elemental staff.
-----Monk-----
Hunting Ability: Very early, while in Horse Stance (HRS), combo
Snapkick, Jab, Jab*; after a few lessons, while in Eagle Stance** (EGS),
combo Sidekick, Uppercut, Uppercut
Defensive Ability: Weathering, Vitality, Toughness, Bodyblock (reduce
physical, Tekura 112 Lessons), Regeneration (heal while draining mana,
Kaido 134 Lessons), Resistance (magic defense, Kaido 267 Lessons)
Weapon: None
Armor: Leather
Shield: None
*Monks fight by chaining combinations together to unleash devastating
combinations of blows called a combo. Typically, these consist of being
in a stance, a kick, and two punches.
**Stances are abilities within Tekura that impact how a monk fights
while in them. For example, the Eagle Stance will make subsequent
punches and kicks faster. While hunting, you should stay in Eagle until
far down the line, when you learn Scorpion.
-----Pariah-----
Hunting Ability: Blood Wrack* until you can trace a cyclical epitaph,
Sting (Pestilence)
Defensive Ability: Shield** (primary defence, keep up at all times)
Weapon: Ritual knife**
Armor: Trace Shield
Shield: None
*When hunting, it can be your own blood. In combat with another
adventurer, you will need their blood through Sanguine Hand. This is
likely irrelevant early on.
**Before beginning, you will need your ritual knife, and blood in it.
This must be done in a desert a"walk to the Mhojave. CRUX DRAW to get
your knife, then CRUX LET ME to get your blood into it, then TRACE
SHIELD ME to get your defence up.
Notes:
--> Upon achieving Charnel, seek someone out for advice.
--> To utilise most of Charnel, you'll need to read the tablet at the
landmark PARIAHTABLET and undergo the ritual in your crypt built in the
desert. The Chamber of Interment is one of the most important.
-----Psion-----
Hunting Ability: Overhand, eventually Deathblow (same damage, both
faster than Charge); eventually Flurry (a finishing blow a"higher
damage, slightly slower speed); Guided Strike (increase criticals) in
Emulation
Defensive Ability: Secondskin, Rally (health)
Weapon: None***
Armor: Cloth****
Shield: Buckler
*Unlocked upon embracing class, then the trip to Nur.
**Transcend fully charges every five attacks. This can be used with
Splinter for a balanceless shield break, or combined with Shatter for
additional damage to denizens.
***Psion abilities create spectral weapons that dissipate after the
attack.
****Secondskin acts as your main armour; cloth is nearly useless.
Notes:
--> Once you embrace this class, you can learn Emulation. However,
learning Emulation also involves taking a pilgrimage to Nur, accessible
by entering the flame at Yggdrasil and climbing up. It is a dangerous
journey a"see the PSIONNUR scroll.
--> To learn Emulation from a denizen, it must be Candlemas in Azdun.
Weaving and Psionics must also be learned from Candlemas after a certain
point if you wish to learn from a denizen. Otherwise, learn from another
adventurer.
-----Serpent-----

Garrote
Defensive Ability: Scales
Weapon: Whip or Lash*
Armor: Scalemail
Shield: Buckler
*Whip is stronger, Lash is faster. Try for a Lash a"speed is better.
